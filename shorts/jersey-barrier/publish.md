## Описание

```
Why do concrete highway barriers have a slope at the bottom? That angled base is the whole trick.

When a car drifts into a Jersey barrier at a shallow angle, the tire hits the lower slope first and climbs it. The car lifts slightly, the tires grip less, and the barrier steers the car back along the wall — instead of the car's body taking the full hit. A flat vertical wall is harsher: a bigger jolt and more crushed metal.

The New Jersey shape: 32 inches tall, a 3-inch vertical lip, then a 55° slope up to 13 inches, then a steep 84° face.

And with no barrier at all? On August 22, 2026, on Route 55 in Vineland, New Jersey — the state that invented the Jersey barrier — a car crossed a grass median into oncoming traffic and killed a 68-year-old man driving an SUV.
```
