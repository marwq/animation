What if you could see everyone's stats... in real life? 📊
Your teacher? Level 99. Final boss. The school bully? Level 80, but his health is almost zero. Your little brother? CHAOS: 9,999. You can see everybody's stats. Everybody's... except yours. ❓

One night a strange update pops up: Reality HUD v1.0. 4,812 pages of terms. You press ACCEPT (nobody reads those).
